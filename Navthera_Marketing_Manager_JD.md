# Marketing Manager — Navthera

**Navthera | Advanced Physio and Rehab Centre**
Jaipur, Rajasthan · On-site · Full-time

---

## About Navthera

Navthera is a next-generation physiotherapy and rehabilitation centre in Jaipur offering specialised services across Neuro Rehab, Aquatherapy, Sports & Ortho, and Comprehensive Rehabilitation. We combine evidence-based clinical protocols with modern technology to deliver measurable patient outcomes — and we're building a brand to match.

We're looking for a **Marketing Manager** who can own and grow our digital presence while also rolling up their sleeves for offline campaigns, community engagement, and doctor-network outreach when the moment calls for it.

---

## What You'll Do

**Digital Marketing & Growth**

- Plan and execute digital campaigns across Google Ads, Meta (Facebook/Instagram), YouTube, and other relevant platforms to drive patient acquisition.
- Own SEO strategy — on-page, technical, and local SEO — to ensure Navthera ranks for high-intent searches in Jaipur and Rajasthan.
- Manage and grow social media channels with consistent, medically credible, and engaging content (reels, carousels, patient stories, educational posts).
- Track and report on key metrics (CAC, ROAS, engagement, lead-to-visit conversion) using Google Analytics, Meta Business Suite, and CRM tools.
- Manage the website — content updates, landing pages, blog, and performance optimisation.

**Content & Brand**

- Develop a content calendar that positions Navthera as a thought leader in rehabilitation and physiotherapy.
- Coordinate with clinical staff to produce patient success stories, treatment explainers, and awareness campaigns (e.g., World Stroke Day, Physiotherapy Day).
- Ensure brand consistency across all touchpoints — digital, print, in-centre signage, and merchandise.

**Beyond Digital**

- Build and maintain referral relationships with doctors, hospitals, and corporate wellness programmes.
- Plan and execute on-ground activities: health camps, community talks, sponsorships, and local PR.
- Support the leadership team with marketing collateral for investor meetings, partnerships, and new centre launches.

- 2–5 years of hands-on marketing experience, ideally with some exposure to healthcare, wellness, or a service-based business.

- Comfortable with analytics dashboards and making data-informed decisions.
- Excellent written and spoken communication in English and Hindi.
- A self-starter who can operate independently in a fast-paced startup environment — you won't have a 10-person team behind you; you'll be the team.
- Willingness to go beyond the screen — attending events, visiting referral partners, and being present in the community.

**Nice to Have**

- Familiarity with CRM or marketing automation platforms (HubSpot, Zoho, etc.).
- Background in healthcare marketing or health-tech.
