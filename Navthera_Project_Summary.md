## 2. Gym & Exercise Equipment — Decisions

### Context
Limited space. Focus on physiotherapy patients (ortho, sports, neuro). Not a commercial gym. Equipment must serve rehab purposes, not aesthetics.

### Final Essential List (Physio-Specific)

| Equipment | Purpose | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Treadmill (ortho/sports)** | Gait training, cardio rehab | Separate neuro treadmill already planned |
| **Recumbent Bike** | Low-impact cardio, post-op knee/hip | Safer than upright for early rehab |
| **Functional Trainer (cable)** | Virtually all muscle groups, ROM exercises | Most versatile machine in physio |
| **Leg Extension / Leg Curl (dual)** | Isolated quad/hamstring strengthening | Critical for knee rehab |
| **Abductor / Adductor (dual)** | Hip stabilisers, post-hip-surgery | Common gap in basic setups |

**Leg press**: Can be skipped initially — functional trainer + leg extension/curl covers it adequately.

### Fitline Brand Assessment
- **Founded:** 1994 (28+ years)
- **Motor:** Commercial AC motors, not consumer DC — important for longevity in clinical use
- **Strength machines:** Selectorized, 96–100kg weight stacks, solid plate steel frames
- **Service:** Pan-India service network — important for Jaipur
- **Verdict:** Solid mid-tier commercial brand appropriate for a physio centre. Not premium (not Life Fitness/Technogym) but significantly better than gym-store consumer brands.

**Recommended Fitline machines for Navthera:**
- Treadmill (ortho/sports)
- Functional Trainer (or Functional Trainer + Smith Machine combo — justified if space allows, eliminates need for separate squat rack)
- Leg Extension/Curl Dual
- Abductor/Adductor Dual

## 3. Cryotherapy — Research & Decisions

### LGT-2410S (Longest Medical, China) — CO2 Cryotherapy
- **Technology:** CO2 gas expanding through nozzle → reaches ~−78°C at point of application
- **Mechanism:** Fundamentally different from dry cold air systems (BTL, Enraf Nonius)
- **Pros:** Very cold, fast treatment times, relatively affordable
- **Cons:** CO2 cylinders = recurring consumable cost + logistics; −78°C requires careful technique to avoid ice burns; limited peer-reviewed RCT data vs. established brands
- **Safety:** Requires training; cannot be applied directly to skin; limited to short burst durations
- **Regulatory (India):** Under CDSCO Medical Devices Rules 2017, the *importer/manufacturer* bears licensing obligation (Form MD-14 / MD-15), not the clinic. Verify supplier has CDSCO clearance before purchasing.

### Comparison: Technology Types

| System | Technology | Temperature | Pros | Cons |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| LGT-2410S (Longest) | CO2 gas | ~−78°C nozzle | Very cold, fast | CO2 consumables, less evidence |
| BTL Cryo | Dry cold air | −30°C to −32°C | Strong clinical evidence, excellent support | Expensive |
| Enraf Nonius | Dry cold air | ~−35°C | Strong evidence, European | Very expensive |
| Cryotur | Dry cold air | ~−40°C | Good evidence | Expensive |
| **Zimmer MedizinSysteme Cryo 6** | Dry cold air | −30°C to −43°C | German quality, CE certified, cheaper than BTL/Enraf, India distributor presence | Still premium pricing |

### Recommendation
**Zimmer Cryo 6** is the strongest value proposition — German-engineered, strong clinical evidence base (same physics as BTL/Enraf), CE certified, and meaningfully cheaper than BTL or Enraf Nonius. Has existing India distributor presence (used in hospitals for anaesthesia/pain).

Chinese dry-air alternatives: a few exist but none have the clinical credibility, evidence base, or India service infrastructure to compete with Zimmer at this stage.

American alternatives: Breg Polar Care, Game Ready — excellent cold compression systems but different product category (targeted cold + compression, not whole-field cryotherapy).

## 4. Pilates Equipment — Decisions

### Source: Shandong Meijinda (China, factory direct)

**Why Maple over Oak:**
- Maple Janka hardness: ~1,450 lbf vs. Oak ~1,290 lbf — meaningfully harder
- Maple has tighter grain → less moisture absorption (critical in a physio setting with sweat)
- Maple surface finishes more consistently
- Price premium ~50–65% on catalog prices, but absolute cost difference is small given factory pricing
- **Verdict: Maple is justified for clinical use**

**Recommended purchases (physio-specific):**

| Item | Code | FOB Price | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Full Track Core Bed (Maple) | MJD-W46A | ~$622 | Most versatile Pilates reformer; essential |
| Cadillac / Trapeze Table (Maple) | MJD-W16A | ~$532 | For spinal, post-op, neurological patients |
| German springs | +$26/machine | Specify at order | Superior durability vs. Chinese springs |

**Items to avoid:**
Anything labelled "Merrithew" in the catalog (MJD-AA06, AA07, AA10, AA12) — these are likely replicas of a Canadian brand's IP. CDSCO/customs risk and potential IP issues.

**Import logistics:**
- HSN Code: 9506 (gym/sports equipment)
- Landed Jaipur cost ≈ FOB China price × 1.6–1.8
- Use LCL (Less than Container Load / groupage) shipping for small quantities
- Appoint a licensed customs broker in Delhi or Jaipur
- Ensure supplier provides commercial invoice, packing list, COO, and any conformity certificates

## 5. Treadmill — Ortho & Sports Use

Evaluated cult.fit / Cultstore treadmill range for ortho and sports rehab use (neuro treadmill planned separately).

**Key requirements for ortho/sports treadmill:**
- Motor: Minimum 3–4HP continuous duty (not peak)
- Speed: 0.5–20 km/h with fine low-speed increments (for gait training)
- Incline: Minimum 15%, ideally with decline option
- Deck size: Minimum 55cm × 150cm
- Shock absorption: Ortho deck cushioning system
- Handrails: Parallel bars preferred over standard user rails for rehab use
- Weight capacity: 150kg+

Cultstore's commercial range (Matrix, Precor, Technogym reseller) is appropriate. Specific model recommendation depends on final budget bracket.
