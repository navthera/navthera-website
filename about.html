The Vision Behind Navthera

We set out to build something Rajasthan had never seen — a rehabilitation centre that brings world-class technology and compassionate care under one roof.

Our Mission

To make advanced rehabilitation accessible to every patient in Rajasthan. We believe that geography should never limit the quality of care someone receives. By bringing cutting-edge technology and evidence-based protocols to Jaipur, we're closing the gap between what's available in metro super-specialty hospitals and what patients in our region deserve.

Our Vision

To establish Navthera as North India's most trusted physiotherapy and rehabilitation destination — where patients don't just recover, but return to life stronger. We envision a future where every individual, regardless of their condition, has access to the technology and expertise that can transform their recovery journey.

Our Story

Built on a Personal Promise

Navthera was born from a simple but powerful observation — patients in Rajasthan were traveling hundreds of kilometres to access rehabilitation technology that should be available locally. Families were uprooted, recovery was delayed, and costs spiralled unnecessarily.

We decided to change that. With a team of passionate physiotherapists and rehabilitation specialists, we designed Navthera from the ground up — not as just another clinic, but as a comprehensive rehabilitation ecosystem. Every room, every piece of equipment, every protocol was chosen with a single question in mind: does this give our patients the best possible chance of recovery?

The result is Jaipur's most advanced physiotherapy centre, complete with Rajasthan's first aquatherapy pool, neuro rehabilitation robotics, cryotherapy suites, and much more.
