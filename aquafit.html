Something New

Exercise Reimagined in Water

AquaFit is not swimming. It's high-performance fitness training that uses the unique properties of water — buoyancy, resistance and pressure — to deliver a workout that works harder than the gym, without the wear and tear.

Whether you're a weekend runner who wants a smarter cross-training day, someone who finds the gym too hard on your joints, or just curious about something different — AquaFit is open to everyone. No medical condition required. Just the will to try something new.

Our pool is equipped with an underwater treadmill, aqua bike, resistance jets and a full set of aquatic exercise equipment. Every session is guided by our trained staff.

Higher calorie burn

Water is 800× denser than air — movement demands significantly more energy, even at lower perceived effort.

No impact, all intensity

Buoyancy reduces body weight on joints by up to 90% — so you can train intensely without soreness or injury risk.

Full-body engagement

Water's multidirectional resistance means stabiliser muscles work constantly — every rep is a full-body effort.
