Understanding Aquatherapy

What is Aquatherapy?

Navthera's state-of-the-art aquatherapy facility provides rehabilitation in a temperature-controlled therapeutic pool designed for safe and comfortable movement. Water reduces body weight and pressure on joints, allowing patients to move more freely with less pain and strain. Our programs include guided exercises for mobility, strength, balance, walking training, post-surgery recovery, neurological rehabilitation, and weight management. Aquatherapy is especially beneficial for patients who cannot tolerate land-based exercises due to pain, weakness, instability, or fear of falling. Each session is supervised by trained therapists who tailor the program to the patient's condition and recovery goals.

Water creates a unique healing environment that cannot be replicated on land. Buoyancy supports the body and reduces joint stress, allowing earlier movement after injury or surgery. Warm water relaxes muscles, improves circulation, and reduces pain, enabling more effective therapy sessions. Natural water resistance strengthens muscles safely without overloading joints. The supportive environment also reduces fall risk, making it ideal for elderly and neurological patients. Because patients can move with less pain and greater confidence, aquatherapy often accelerates recovery, improves mobility and balance, enhances endurance, and helps individuals return to daily activities faster than conventional therapy alone.

At Navthera, our aquatherapy pool represents a significant investment in bringing this world-class treatment modality to Rajasthan for the first time, ensuring that patients in Jaipur and surrounding regions no longer need to travel to metro cities for water-based rehabilitation.

The Science

How Water Heals

Buoyancy

Water supports body weight, reducing joint load by up to 90%. Patients can move freely with significantly less pain and stress on healing tissues.

Hydrostatic Pressure

Water pressure uniformly compresses the body, reducing swelling, improving circulation, and providing proprioceptive feedback for balance recovery.

Viscosity

Water's natural resistance provides gentle, graded strengthening. Movement speed determines effort — patients control their own intensity naturally.

Thermal Effects

Warm water (33–36°C) relaxes muscles, reduces pain perception, increases blood flow, and creates an analgesic environment for comfortable exercise.

Who Benefits

Conditions Treated with Aquatherapy

Orthopaedic Conditions

Water reduces body weight and joint stress, enabling pain-free movement and earlier rehabilitation for musculoskeletal conditions.

  • Slip disc / Herniated disc
  • Sciatica
  • Chronic back and neck pain
  • Osteoarthritis
  • Rheumatoid arthritis
  • Ankylosing spondylitis
  • Avascular necrosis (AVN)
  • Fibromyalgia
  • Joint stiffness and mobility loss
  • Post-fracture stiffness
  • Post joint-replacement rehabilitation

Neurological Conditions

Water buoyancy allows neurological patients to attempt movements that are impossible on land, accelerating neuroplastic recovery.

  • Stroke and hemiparesis
  • Spinal cord injury
  • Cerebral palsy, Autism, ADHD
  • Multiple sclerosis
  • Traumatic brain injury
  • Parkinson's disease
  • Guillain-Barré syndrome (recovery phase)
  • Peripheral neuropathy
  • Gait ataxia and walking disorders
  • Balance disorders

Sports & Injury Rehabilitation

Athletes use aquatic rehabilitation for early-stage recovery and return-to-sport conditioning with reduced reinjury risk.

  • ACL rehabilitation
  • Knee ligament injuries
  • Ankle instability
  • Muscle injuries
  • Stress injuries
  • Post-surgical sports recovery

Geriatric & Mobility Issues

The supportive aquatic environment reduces fall risk, making it ideal for elderly patients to exercise with confidence.

  • Age-related weakness
  • Joint pain in elderly
  • Difficulty walking
  • Fear of falling
  • Balance problems

Weight Loss & Fitness

Water provides resistance and buoyancy for effective, low-impact exercise ideal for patients who cannot exercise on land.

  • Obesity-related joint pain
  • Low-impact weight loss programs
  • Fitness for individuals unable to exercise on land

Our Facility

Pool Features & Specifications

Temperature Controlled

Maintained between 33–36°C for optimal therapeutic benefit. Warm enough for muscle relaxation, comfortable for extended sessions.

Variable Depth

Adjustable depth zones allow progressive weight-bearing from chest-deep (minimal load) to waist-deep (moderate load) exercises.

Underwater Treadmill

Built-in aquatic treadmill for gait training with buoyancy support — ideal for neurological and post-surgical walking rehab.

Resistance Jets

Directional water jets provide adjustable resistance for strengthening exercises and functional movement training.

Accessibility Ramp & Hoist

Wheelchair-accessible ramp and patient hoist ensure safe entry for patients with severe mobility limitations.

Hygiene & Filtration

Advanced UV and ozone filtration system maintains clinical hygiene standards, gentle on skin and suitable for all patients.
