Balance & Vestibular

Restore balance, reduce dizziness, and move with confidence.

Balance and vestibular rehabilitation helps patients with vertigo, dizziness, unsteadiness, and fall risk regain stability through targeted exercises and progressive training.

Book Balance Assessment Back to Services

Common concerns

  • Vertigo and BPPV
  • Chronic dizziness and motion sensitivity
  • Balance problems after stroke or neuropathy
  • Fear of falling and gait insecurity

What the programme includes

We use balance drills, visual-vestibular training, strength work, gait practice, and environmental challenge exercises to improve confidence in real-life movement.

  • Improve head and eye coordination.
  • Strengthen balance reactions and postural control.
  • Reduce fall risk and improve walking confidence.

Why it matters

Many patients feel they must avoid walking, driving, or exercise because of dizziness. Our approach helps restore safe movement and independence.
