Cardio & Respiratory

Improve breathing, endurance, and quality of life.

Cardio and respiratory rehabilitation helps patients recover after heart or lung disease, surgery, or prolonged illness by improving breath control, stamina, and functional capacity.

Book Cardio Consultation Back to Services

Conditions we support

  • Chronic breathlessness and COPD
  • Post-heart surgery and cardiac events
  • Reduced stamina and deconditioning
  • Chest clearance and breathing control

What therapy focuses on

We build a safe and progressive exercise plan that improves breathing efficiency, endurance, and confidence with daily activities.

  • Breathing retraining and airway clearance.
  • Progressive cardio conditioning.
  • Energy management for daily living.

Expected benefits

Many patients notice less breathlessness, better stamina, and improved ability to walk, climb stairs, and carry out daily tasks.
