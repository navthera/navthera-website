Geriatric Rehab

Gentle rehabilitation that helps older adults stay active and independent.

Our geriatric rehabilitation service focuses on pain reduction, safe mobility, fall prevention, and recovery after surgery or illness so seniors can keep doing the things that matter to them.

Book Geriatric Consultation Back to Services

Needs we support

  • Joint pain and arthritis
  • Walking difficulty and falls
  • Post-surgery recovery
  • Balance, weakness, and reduced endurance

Our focus

We create compassionate, low-stress rehabilitation plans that improve mobility and confidence without overwhelming the patient.

  • Improve strength and balance safely.
  • Support pain-free movement and everyday tasks.
  • Use aquatherapy and gentle exercise where appropriate.

What this can improve

From climbing stairs to getting out of bed, our goal is to help seniors feel stronger, steadier, and more independent.

Need support for ageing-related mobility or pain?

Book a geriatric assessment and let us tailor a plan that supports comfort, safety, and confidence.
