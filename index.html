Aquatherapy — Healing Through Water

Whether you're recovering from injury or surgery, or simply looking for a new way to train — our purpose-built therapeutic pool offers something powerful for everyone.

For Rehabilitation

Recover Faster.
Move Without Pain.

Water reduces up to 90% of body weight on joints — allowing patients to begin active therapy weeks earlier, regain movement sooner, and heal faster than land-based treatment alone.

  • Orthopaedic & post-surgical rehabilitation
  • Neurological recovery — stroke, SCI, brain injury
  • Sports injuries & return-to-sport
  • Chronic pain, elderly care & paediatrics

AquaFit — For Everyone

Train Differently.
Perform Better.

You don't need to be a patient to train here. Our underwater treadmill, aqua bike and resistance equipment offer a completely new kind of workout — high intensity, zero joint impact.

  • Underwater treadmill & aqua bike
  • Water resistance training — full body
  • Maximum calorie burn, no joint stress
  • Individual sessions or 30-session packs

World-Class Infrastructure

Advanced Equipment & Technology

Our centre houses technology typically found only in premier rehabilitation hospitals worldwide.

Cryotherapy

Localised cold therapy for rapid pain relief, inflammation control, and accelerated tissue recovery.

Shockwave Therapy

Radial and focused shockwave for chronic tendinopathies, calcifications, and stubborn soft tissue injuries.

LASER Therapy

High-intensity Class IV laser delivering deep tissue healing and targeted inflammation reduction.

Radiofrequency — TECAR

Capacitive and resistive energy transfer to stimulate cellular repair and restore deep tissue function.

Virtual Reality Therapy

Immersive VR environments for neurological rehab, balance retraining, and motor coordination recovery.

FMS Chair

Functional magnetic stimulation for non-invasive pelvic floor strengthening and incontinence treatment.

FES — Functional Electrical Stimulation

Electrical impulses that activate paralysed or weakened muscles to restore voluntary movement.

Aquatic Treadmill

Underwater treadmill within our purpose-built therapeutic pool for buoyancy-assisted gait and endurance training.

Robotic Decompression

Automated spinal traction and decompression for disc herniations, sciatica, and chronic back pain.

EMS / NMES Units

Neuromuscular electrical stimulation for muscle re-education, strength rebuilding, and atrophy prevention.
