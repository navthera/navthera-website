Rajasthan's First

Aquatherapy — Healing Through Water

Our state-of-the-art aquatherapy pool is specifically designed for therapeutic rehabilitation, offering buoyancy-assisted treatment that accelerates recovery for a wide range of conditions.

First in Rajasthan

Neurological Recovery

Warm water buoyancy reduces gravity's impact, allowing stroke and spinal cord injury patients to regain movement patterns safely and progressively.

Orthopaedic Rehab

Post-surgical patients recover faster with water-based resistance training. Joint replacements, fractures, and ligament repairs benefit from reduced weight-bearing stress.

Sports Injuries

Athletes return to peak performance through aquatic plyometrics, resistance running, and functional movement restoration in our temperature-controlled pool.

Cardiac Rehabilitation

Water's hydrostatic pressure supports cardiovascular conditioning with lower cardiac stress, ideal for patients recovering from cardiac events.

Chronic Pain Management

Warm water therapy provides natural analgesic effects, reducing chronic pain from fibromyalgia, arthritis, and persistent musculoskeletal conditions.

Paediatric Therapy

Children with cerebral palsy, developmental delays, and genetic conditions benefit from the supportive aquatic environment for motor skill development.
