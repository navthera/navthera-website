Neuro Rehab

Rebuild movement, confidence, and independence after neurological injury.

Our neurological rehabilitation programme combines robotic-assisted therapy, virtual reality, balance training, and water-based recovery to help patients regain strength, coordination, and functional independence.

What rehabilitation looks like

Neuro Rehab is not just about exercise; it is about retraining the brain and body to move with purpose again. We use progressive, goal-based therapy so every session builds toward daily life.

How we help

  • Improve walking, standing balance, and coordination.
  • Reduce spasticity, weakness, and fear of movement.
  • Support hand function, posture, and transfers.
  • Help patients return to home, work, and daily routines.

Conditions we treat

Major neurological conditions

  • Stroke (paralysis, weakness, speech & balance issues)
  • Spinal cord injury (paralysis, walking difficulty)
  • Traumatic brain injury (head injury recovery)
  • Parkinson's disease
  • Multiple sclerosis
  • Cerebral palsy
  • Bell's Palsy

Neuromuscular and nerve conditions

  • Peripheral nerve injuries
  • Neuropathies (including diabetic neuropathy)
  • Guillain-Barré syndrome
  • Motor neuron disease (ALS)
  • Muscular dystrophy and myopathies
  • Polyneuropathy

Brain, spinal and movement concerns

  • Brain tumor or neurosurgery recovery
  • Spinal surgery recovery
  • Transverse myelitis
  • Functional neurological disorders
  • Ataxia and coordination disorders
  • Difficulty walking or standing
  • Balance disorders and frequent falls
  • Tremors, postural instability, weakness or loss of coordination

Paediatric, cognitive and functional support

  • Developmental delay
  • Dyspraxia
  • Paediatric movement disorders
  • Dementia-related mobility decline
  • Age-related neurological weakness
  • Neurogenic bladder and bowel dysfunction
  • Loss of independence in daily activities

Brain relearning

Intensive, repetitive training helps the brain rebuild neural pathways through neuroplasticity — the key to neurological recovery.

Earlier mobilisation

Robotic support enables walking and standing training even for patients who cannot move independently.

Pain-free practice

Water therapy and VR create safe, engaging environments where patients move without fear of falling or joint stress.

Faster independence

Combined technologies increase therapy intensity, improving walking, hand use, balance, and daily activity function.
