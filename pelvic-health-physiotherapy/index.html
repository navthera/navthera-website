Pelvic Health

Support for bladder, bowel, and pelvic floor concerns.

Our pelvic health programmes offer non-invasive, evidence-based care for urinary incontinence, pelvic pain, postpartum recovery, and men’s and women’s pelvic floor dysfunction.

Book Pelvic Health Consultation Back to Services

Common concerns we treat

  • Urinary leakage and urgency
  • Postpartum pelvic weakness
  • Pelvic pain and prolapse
  • Men’s pelvic health and post-prostate recovery

How therapy helps

We use magnetic stimulation and guided exercise therapy to retrain the pelvic floor muscles gently and effectively when traditional exercises alone are not enough.

  • Improve bladder control and reduce urgency.
  • Support postnatal and post-surgical recovery.
  • Reduce discomfort and increase confidence in daily life.

Why patients choose us

Our approach is private, comfortable, and tailored. Patients receive clear education, measurable goals, and treatment that respects their comfort and personal needs.
